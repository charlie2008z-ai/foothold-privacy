# Foothold Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

Foothold is an iPhone app for keeping one small promise at a time. It is published on the App Store by Sean McElligott ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Foothold.

## The short version

We don't collect any of your data. Everything you put into Foothold stays on your iPhone. We have no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, so there is nothing for us to see, sell or share.

## What Foothold stores, and where

Foothold keeps the following **only on your device**:

- **Your promises, summits and reflections:** the words you type, with their dates and whether each promise was kept.
- **Your climb history:** your height, streaks and past climbs.
- **Your photos:** the photo you take or choose to show a promise was kept. Photos are compressed and saved inside the app's private storage, not in your photo library.
- **Settings and subscription status:** your reminder preferences, and a saved copy of whether your subscription is active so the app and its widgets work offline.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Foothold does not connect to any server of ours.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple may include Foothold's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't access it.

## Camera and photos

Foothold asks for camera access only so you can photograph a kept promise. If you choose a picture from your library instead, iOS lets you pick a single photo and Foothold only receives that photo. Foothold never reads the rest of your library.

## Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. Widgets and Live Activities show your current promise on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. None of this goes through our servers.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email, Apple ID or payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Sharing

If you share a photo, climb film or summit from Foothold, iOS opens its standard share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Once shared, it is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

## Third parties

Foothold contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

To delete everything Foothold stored, delete the app from your iPhone. That removes your promises, photos and history. Because we never had a copy, there is nothing else to delete.

## Children

Foothold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and change the effective date above. If Foothold ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the app before it happens.
